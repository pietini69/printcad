Frequently Asked Questions

Which macOS versions are supported?

PrintCAD is built natively for macOS 14 Sonoma and macOS 15 Sequoia. It runs natively on all Apple Silicon chips (M1, M2, M3, M4) as well as Intel Macs.

How does payment and license delivery work?

Payment is processed securely via PayPal (accepts all debit/credit cards & PayPal). Your license key (e.g. PCAD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) is emailed directly to your PayPal address. For instant help or questions, contact Niko at niko.pietinen@gmail.com.

Can I install PrintCAD on multiple Macs?

Yes. A single personal license allows you to activate PrintCAD across all your personal Macs (e.g. desktop Mac Studio and MacBook Pro).

Will it work with my 3D printer?

Yes! PrintCAD exports clean, standard Binary STL files compatible with every 3D slicer on the market (OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura, Lychee, Simplify3D).
